Privacy Policy

EasyMath Safari

Effective Date: February 21, 2026

1. Introduction

EasyMath Safari ("EasyMath", "we", "our", or "us") is an educational application designed to assist users with mathematics, document scanning, text recognition, AI-based explanations, and learning tools.

By using EasyMath, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

If users create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address

This information is used only to manage accounts and provide subscription features. We do not sell personal data.

3. AI & Internet-Based Features

Certain features (AI Help, Math Assistant, Translator) require an internet connection.

User input may be securely transmitted to our cloud server (Google Cloud, EU region) for processing. Data is processed only to generate the requested response.

We do not permanently store user conversations.

4. Camera & Microphone

Camera access is used for document scanning and math recognition.

Microphone access is used for voice input features.

Media and audio are processed only to provide requested functionality and are not permanently stored.

5. Payments & Subscriptions

All payments are processed via Apple's In-App Purchase system. EasyMath does not store payment information.

6. Advertisements

Free plans may include video advertisements. Third-party ad networks may process limited device-related data under their own privacy policies.

7. Local Data Storage

User notes and generated content are stored locally on the user's device unless exported by the user.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect user data and secure communication.

9. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without required parental consent.

10. Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
